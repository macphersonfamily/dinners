Saturday Nov 21

family · American
Traeger burgers on whole-wheat buns with lettuce, tomato and onion, plus carrot fries.
  • Cook: Freezer burger patties go on the Traeger frozen — no thaw. Family night; Matt takes 2 patties.
  • Prep/Season: Salt and pepper the patties. Slice onion, tomato, and lettuce for the buns.
  • Cook: Traeger 400°F, about 18–22 min from frozen, flipping once, to 160°F.
  • Sides: Carrot fries: cut 1.5 lb carrots into sticks; toss with 1 tsp oil, salt, and garlic powder. Roast at 425°F for 20–25 min, flipping halfway (or on the Traeger beside the burgers).
  • Serve: On whole-wheat buns with lettuce, tomato, and onion. Matt ~40–50 g.

Sunday Nov 22

family · BBQ
Traeger chicken breasts with a light BBQ glaze, corn on the cob, and light coleslaw.
  • Prep/Season: Pat dry 2.5 lb chicken breasts. Rub all of it with salt, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder — no sauce yet.
  • Cook: Traeger 375°F, about 25–35 min to 165°F. For the 1.5 lb served tonight, brush a light BBQ glaze on for the last 5–8 min.
  • Set aside: Keep 1 lb rub-only (no sauce) in the fridge for Wednesday wraps.
  • Sides: 6 ears of corn: Traeger 15–20 min, turning, or boil 6–8 min. Light coleslaw: toss the bagged mix with 2 Tbsp light mayo or yogurt, 1 Tbsp vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  • Serve: Matt ~50 g.

Monday Nov 23

gym · Italian · kids 5:30 / adults 9
Crockpot turkey meat sauce (half batch) over whole-wheat spaghetti, side salad. Same pot 5:30 and 9.
  • Thaw: 1 lb frozen ground turkey (the leftover saved Thursday) to the fridge Sunday night.
  • Prep: In the morning, crumble the turkey into the crockpot with 1 can (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes, 1 chopped onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1½ tsp Italian seasoning, 1 tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper.
  • Cook: Crockpot low 6–7 hr or high 3–4 hr to 165°F; keep on warm for both sittings.
  • Sides: At each sitting, boil about 2 oz dry whole-wheat spaghetti per person in salted water (package time). Bagged salad (pea-free, no melon).
  • Serve: Kids 5:30, adults 9. Matt ~45 g.

Tuesday Nov 24

gym · Asian · Miles at 5:30 / adults 9
Crockpot honey-garlic pork tenderloin, steamed green beans, and jasmine rice. Same pot 5:30 and 9.
  • Prep/Season: After the Tuesday shop, put the 1–1.25 lb pork tenderloin in the crockpot with 2 Tbsp honey, 2 Tbsp pea-free soy sauce, and 2 minced garlic cloves.
  • Cook: Crockpot low 4–5 hr, check at 3 hr, to 145°F; rest 5 min and slice. Keep on warm, or refrigerate slices and reheat with a splash of sauce to 165°F for the later sitting.
  • Sides: Steam ¾ lb green beans 5–7 min. Cook 1 cup dry jasmine rice per the package (simmer ~15 min, rest 10).
  • Serve: Miles can handle 5:30; adults 9. Matt ~45 g.

Wednesday Nov 25

gym · American · 5:30 / 9
Chicken Caesar wraps from Sunday’s reserved chicken, Caesar kit, whole-wheat tortillas, and baby carrots.
  • Prep: Chop the 1 lb reserved Sunday chicken (rub-only, no BBQ sauce). Reheat in a skillet with a splash of water to 165°F.
  • Cook: Warm the whole-wheat tortillas. Toss the chicken with the Caesar kit (check the dressing for peas) and wrap.
  • Sides: Baby carrots.
  • Serve: Kids 5:30; adults fill fresh or reheat, with the chicken at 165°F, around 9. Matt ~50 g.
