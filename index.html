Saturday Nov 14

family · Greek
Traeger beef kofta, tzatziki, cucumber-tomato salad, warm pita.
  • Thaw: 1.5 lb freezer ground beef, fridge Friday night.
  • Prep/Season: Mix with 2 minced garlic cloves, 1½ tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, and 1 Tbsp oil. Form into 5–6 logs, about 4–5 oz each; thread onto skewers (or oval patties on a grill mat or foil — loose bits fall through the grates).
  • Cook: Traeger 400°F, about 14–18 min, turning once, to 160°F.
  • Sauce: Tzatziki: 1 cup plain Greek yogurt, 1 grated cucumber squeezed dry, 1 minced garlic clove, juice of ½ lemon, and salt.
  • Sides: Dice the other 2 cucumbers and 3 tomatoes; toss with 1 tsp oil, salt, and a squeeze of lemon. Warm the pita, wrapped in foil, on the Traeger for the last 2–3 min.
  • Serve: Matt ~45–50 g.

Sunday Nov 15

family · American
Crockpot picnic pork roast with potatoes and carrots, roasted green beans.
  • Thaw: Picnic roast to the fridge Saturday night. If still icy Sunday morning, thaw in cold water; never start the crockpot frozen.
  • Prep: Whole roast with 2 lb potatoes (halved if large), 1 lb carrots (cut in chunks), 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, and ½ cup water.
  • Cook: Crockpot low 8–10 hr or high 4–5 hr until shreddable, about 195–205°F.
  • Sides: Toss ¾ lb green beans with 1 tsp oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 425°F for 15–18 min.
  • Set aside: Serve about 1.5 lb pork tonight. Chill 1.5 lb shredded pork, salt and pepper only (no other seasoning), for Wednesday. Freeze any remainder in labeled 1 lb bags. Matt ~45 g.

Monday Nov 16

gym · Mexican night
Crockpot chicken tortilla soup, bagged salad, crisp tortilla-strip topping. Same pot 5:30 and 9.
  • Thaw: Fajita-strip bag to the fridge Sunday night.
  • Prep: In the morning, add the thawed strips, 2 cans pinto beans (drained; check labels for peas), 2 cans diced tomatoes, 32 oz pea-free chicken broth, 1 chopped onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1½ tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, and 1 tsp salt.
  • Cook: Crockpot low 6–7 hr or high 3–4 hr to 165°F; keep on warm for both sittings.
  • Topping: Cut the corn tortillas into strips, toss with 1 tsp oil and a pinch of salt, bake at 400°F for 8–10 min until crisp, and add them on top of the soup (not a side of warm tortillas).
  • Sides: Bagged salad (pea-free, no melon). Matt ~45 g.

Tuesday Nov 17

gym · Italian · 2A Caucus to 6 PM
Crockpot chicken cacciatore (no mushrooms), whole-wheat penne, Caesar salad kit. Kids boil pasta at 5:30; adults reheat.
  • Thaw: 1.5 lb freezer chicken thighs to the fridge Monday night; do not buy more.
  • Prep: In the morning, put the thawed thighs in the crockpot with 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes, 1 chopped onion, 1 sliced bell pepper, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1½ tsp Italian seasoning, 1 tsp salt, and ½ tsp pepper. No mushrooms.
  • Cook: Crockpot low 6–7 hr or high 3–4 hr to 165–175°F; keep on warm.
  • Sides: At 5:30, boil 12–16 oz whole-wheat penne in salted water (package time); toss with the cacciatore and reserve half, covered, for the later sitting — reheat that pasta with a splash of sauce to 165°F. Caesar kit (check the dressing for peas). Matt ~50 g.

Wednesday Nov 18

gym · Cuban-style
Pressed pork sandwiches from Sunday’s reserved plain pork, mustard, pickles, light Swiss, roasted broccoli.
  • Prep: Reheat the 1.5 lb reserved salt-and-pepper shredded pork in a skillet with a splash of water to 165°F.
  • Cook: Split 2 sandwich rolls; spread mustard, pile on the pork, add dill pickle slices and a slice or two of light Swiss. Press in a skillet or panini press (medium, 2–3 min, lightly oiled or dry) until the cheese melts and the pork is 165°F.
  • Sides: Toss ¾ lb broccoli with 1 tsp oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 425°F for 18–22 min.
  • Serve: Matt ~45 g.

Thursday Nov 19

gym · Brian Regan show 7–9
Teriyaki-ginger turkey bowls with stir-fried peppers and broccoli slaw, jasmine rice. Kids cook at 5:30; adults eat with them or reheat ~9:30.
  • Prep/Season: Brown 2 lb 93% ground turkey in a large skillet, breaking it up, with 2 minced garlic cloves and 1 Tbsp grated fresh ginger; stir in ¼ cup pea-free teriyaki.
  • Cook: Turkey to 165°F. Cook 1 cup dry jasmine rice per the package (simmer ~15 min, rest 10).
  • Sides: Slice 2 bell peppers and stir-fry in 1 tsp oil, 5–7 min, until tender-crisp; toss the 10–12 oz plain broccoli slaw in for the last 1–2 min (no bottled slaw dressing).
  • Serve: Kids plate at 5:30 over rice. Adults can eat then, or refrigerate portions and reheat, covered, with a splash of water to 165°F around 9:30. Matt ~50 g.

Friday Nov 20

family · Matt home
Lemon-herb fish, roasted Brussels sprouts, roasted baby potatoes.
  • Thaw: Freezer halibut to the fridge Thursday night. If under about 1.25 lb, add the 1.25 lb freezer cod (thawed the same way).
  • Prep/Season: Pat the fillets dry; leave them whole. Rub with 1 Tbsp oil, 2 minced garlic cloves, juice and zest of 1 lemon, 1 Tbsp chopped parsley or dill, ½ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper.
  • Cook: Bake at 400°F, about 10–14 min depending on thickness, to 145°F.
  • Sides: Halve 1.5 lb baby potatoes, toss with 1 tsp oil and salt, and roast at 425°F for 25–30 min. Halve 1 lb Brussels sprouts, toss with 1 tsp oil and salt, and roast at 425°F for 20–25 min (start the potatoes first, or use two pans). Matt ~50 g.
